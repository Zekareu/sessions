DEEPTHROAT PRISON

Story

You have been imprisoned for a crime you did not commit. In your country, the corruption of the judicial and police systems is widespread, causing many innocent citizens to suffer. The prison where you are being held is a special one, and all the guards are futanari. The sentence handed down to you is to serve their cocks with your throat. Looking back, even the judge who presided over your trial was a futanari. With a sense of looming conspiracy, you contemplate the dark future ahead of you.

Rules & Goal

Your Goal: Roll 1d6 at the start of your sentence to determine the points required for release (550 to 800 pts).

Tasks: Roll 1–6 and perform the sensor-tracked task. Succeed to earn its points. Fail or abort, and the same amount of points is deducted from your total (plus punishment).

Punishment Cell: If you roll 6 on Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced tiers, you are sent to the Punishment Cell, where you must roll 1–6 and complete re-education tasks until you roll a Release instruction.
